02 / META ADS
Funnel Architecture
Full-Funnel Restructure from Single Campaign
All spend was going into a single lead gen campaign targeting cold audiences — no retargeting, no audience segmentation. Rebuilt the structure into three stages: cold broad + interest for top of funnel, warm retargeting of video viewers and engagers at mid, and urgency-led creatives for hot audiences who had visited but not converted. Lead quality ratio improved sharply.

03 / GOOGLE ADS
Channel Strategy
Search vs Demand Gen in a Low-Volume Market
Running Google Ads for a Rishikesh real estate project with near-zero search volume. Search CPL was ₹723 but qualification was 80% — buyers who searched had genuine intent. Demand Gen CPL was ₹357 but qualification was lower. Solved for both: preserved Search for high-intent buyers while weighting 80% of budget toward Demand Gen for volume. Used qualification data from the sales team to govern allocation — not CPL alone.

How I Work
Every campaign starts
with the data layer.
01
Audit before touching
I verify tracking integrity before any optimisation. A broken pixel or duplicate event fires will make a healthy campaign look like a disaster. I fix the measurement before I fix the media.
02
Build the signal
Server-side CAPI, GTM containers, downstream lead quality events — I build the infrastructure that tells Meta and Google what a real buyer looks like, not just a form-filler.
03
Structure the funnel
Cold, warm, hot — each stage has a different job, a different creative, and a different CPL target. I don't run one campaign and hope for the best.
04
Optimise toward revenue
I coordinate with sales teams weekly on lead quality. CPL is a proxy metric. The real question is: how many leads turned into site visits, and how many turned into bookings?
