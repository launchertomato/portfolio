Meta Ads · GTM · Tracking
Server-side CAPI rebuild
Meta's browser pixel was losing signal post-iOS 14 — CPL was inflated, the algorithm was optimising toward form-fillers who ghosted the sales team. Built a full server-side CAPI setup via GTM container, passing downstream lead quality events directly to Meta. The algorithm started finding actual buyers, not just form completers.

Meta Ads · Funnel Strategy
Single campaign → 3-stage full funnel
All spend was going into a single lead gen campaign targeting cold audiences — no retargeting, no audience segmentation. Rebuilt the structure into three stages: cold broad + interest (top), warm retargeting of video viewers and page engagers (mid), and urgency-led creatives for hot audiences (bottom). Lead quality improved sharply because bottom-funnel leads had self-selected through multiple touchpoints.

Google Ads · Channel Strategy
Search vs Demand Gen in a low-volume market
Rishikesh real estate had near-zero search volume. Search CPL was ₹723 but lead qualification was 80% — genuine intent. Demand Gen CPL was ₹357 at lower qualification. Solved by preserving Search for high-intent buyers while weighting 80% of budget toward Demand Gen for volume. Allocation was governed by sales-team qualification data, not CPL alone.
